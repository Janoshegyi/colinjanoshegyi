Professional Profile

A brief overview of my background, experience, and interests.

Living across different countries taught me how people think, choose, and connect. That curiosity eventually led me to marketing, where understanding human behavior is just as important as understanding algorithms.

Digital Marketer, SEO Specialist & Front-End Developer

Today, I focus on SEO, content strategy, and web development. I enjoy building websites that are fast, accessible, and genuinely useful for the people who visit them. My work combines technical implementation with marketing strategy, helping brands improve their visibility across search engines and AI-powered platforms.

My goal is simple: help websites become more visible, more useful, and easier to discover.

I am particularly interested in the future of search, including Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), entity-based SEO, and AI-driven search experiences. As search engines and AI assistants increasingly shape how people find information, I enjoy exploring how brands can adapt and remain discoverable.

My academic background at the University of Szeged provided a foundation in business strategy, consumer behavior, and marketing principles that continue to influence my approach to SEO and digital marketing today.
