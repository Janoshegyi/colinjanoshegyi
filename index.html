Digital Marketer, SEO Specialist & Front-End Developer

Today, I focus on SEO, content strategy, and web development. I enjoy building websites that are fast, accessible, and genuinely useful for the people who visit them. My work combines technical implementation with marketing strategy, helping brands improve their visibility across search engines and AI-powered platforms.

I’m particularly interested in the future of search, including Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), entity-based SEO, and AI-driven search experiences. As search engines and AI assistants increasingly shape how people find information, I’m interested in how brands can remain discoverable as the rules of discovery change.

My academic background in business administration and marketing at the University of Szeged continues to shape how I approach this work, particularly when it comes to strategy, consumer behavior, and understanding why people make the choices they do.
