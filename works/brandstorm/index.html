Born in Roma

Personalized Expressive Fragrance Ecosystem

A reimagined fragrance experience for Valentino — merging AI-powered personalization, AR social sharing, and seamless omnichannel discovery to reach a Gen Z audience that craves identity and emotional connection.

01
The Problem

Traditional fragrance retail is passive and non-interactive. Gen Z demands personalization, identity, and emotional resonance that current experiences fail to deliver.

02
The Solution

A digital-physical ecosystem where users build a personal scent identity profile and receive AI-curated layering recommendations in-store and on mobile.

03
The Impact

Higher engagement, shareable UGC moments, and Valentino positioned as the definitive Gen Z-relevant fragrance house — scalable across L'Oréal brands.
